WalletSlice Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

WalletSlice is published under the name ccm, based in India. For privacy questions, contact ccm.admin.desk@gmail.com, our dedicated WalletSlice support address.

This policy explains how the WalletSlice Android app handles information. WalletSlice helps you record finances, manage budgets and bill reminders, and optionally back up your records to your Google Drive.

Information stored on your device

WalletSlice stores the information you enter or approve, including:

  • Transaction amounts, types, dates, categories, account labels and transaction notes.
  • Budgets, credit limits, bill reminders, and personal notes.
  • Profile name and email, if supplied, and app preferences.
  • Details needed to review detected financial alerts, such as amount, alert type, source app, masked account/card ending, suggested account and due date.
  • Local mappings and rules you approve for matching alerts to your accounts, plus identifiers used to prevent duplicate entries and reminders.

These records support the app's financial summaries and reminders. WalletSlice does not operate a server receiving your financial records. The current app does not include developer-operated advertising or usage-analytics services.

Optional notification access

After you accept the in-app disclosure and enable Android notification access, WalletSlice can receive notification content while running in the background, including when the app is not open. Notifications can originate from banking, payment, messaging and other apps. This access can expose unrelated notification content as well as financial alerts.

WalletSlice examines notification titles and text on your device to identify financial alerts. It filters out unsupported alerts and alerts matching rejection rules, including common OTP and unsuccessful-payment wording. Automated detection is not perfect.

Expenses and payment acknowledgements require your review. After you confirm a card statement and explicitly enable a matching rule, clear statements matching that rule may create bill reminders automatically. Uncertain statements remain in review. Creating a bill reminder does not make a payment.

New pending captures retain structured fields and duplicate-detection identifiers, not the full original notification text. Pending captures and device-specific trusted rules are excluded from the app's backups. Financial records saved after review, and automatic bill reminders, can be included in backups.

Older records imported from earlier app versions may contain notification or message text in their notes. Existing notes are preserved and included in backups unless you edit or delete them.

You can use manual entry without notification access. You can disable capture in WalletSlice Settings or revoke access in Android Settings. Disabling stops new captures, including trusted automatic bills, without erasing previously saved records or pending reviews. Consent is recorded locally with the disclosure version and is excluded from portable backups. You can also pause or reset automatic bill rules in WalletSlice Settings.

The current app does not request direct access to your SMS inbox or call history. A messaging app's notification may nevertheless contain SMS content that notification access makes available.

Local backup and optional Google Drive backup

WalletSlice saves an app-private local backup on your phone. It may also retain a snapshot from before a restore. These files are part of the app's private storage; they are not an independent backup if that storage is erased.

If you select a Google account and enable Drive backup, WalletSlice sends a snapshot directly to Google Drive's application-data storage for that account. It includes saved transactions and notes, budgets, categories, bills, account labels, credit limits, duplicate-detection identifiers and portable settings, including profile name and email if present.

WalletSlice uses the selected account to authorize backup and restore. It requests access to its application-data storage rather than general access to all your Drive files. The app can read those backups to restore your data. Google handles its services under Google's Privacy Policy.

WalletSlice's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Automatic backups target the chosen schedule at 2 AM local time. Android or connectivity conditions can delay execution. A cloud upload can wait for an allowed connection and retry later. You can change the frequency, mobile-data preference or selected account in Backup & restore.

The current backup routine attempts to retain the newest three JSON cloud snapshots after successful upload. Cleanup can fail, and older legacy database backups are retained, so more copies may remain. Turning backup off does not delete existing backups.

Exports and support

When you export or share a report, the information included in that report goes to the destination or app you choose. Copies held by that destination are governed by its practices.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send through the email provider. Avoid sending passwords, OTPs, full card details or financial records that are not necessary for your request.

We delete support correspondence within 12 months after the last reply, unless an unresolved request or a legal obligation requires longer retention. Any correspondence retained for those reasons is deleted when it is no longer needed. You may request earlier deletion by emailing ccm.admin.desk@gmail.com, subject to those exceptions. The developer cannot remotely inspect or erase your app-private financial records or your personal Drive backups.

Security and device authentication

Drive communication uses encrypted HTTPS connections. WalletSlice does not currently add its own end-to-end encryption to its JSON backup files. App-private storage and optional device authentication help restrict local access, but app lock does not separately encrypt every record or exported file.

If you enable app lock, Android performs device authentication. WalletSlice does not receive your fingerprint or face template. Protect your phone, Google account and exported files.

Google authorization and other platform services may handle account and technical information under their own privacy policies to provide and protect those services. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them or erase WalletSlice's app storage. Pending captures remain until reviewed, dismissed or app data is cleared. Duplicate-detection records can remain after a bill is paid or deleted so the same alert does not recreate it.

Deleting a record does not immediately remove it from every previous backup, pre-restore snapshot or exported report. A restored old backup can bring previously deleted records back.

To remove all local WalletSlice data, Android's app-storage controls can clear the app's storage. This also removes the app-private local backup, settings and capture rules. This operation is destructive.

Cloud backups must be removed separately using Google Drive's controls for WalletSlice's hidden application data. Stop automatic backup before removing cloud copies to prevent a later scheduled upload creating another copy. Revoking Google access alone is not a guarantee that previously stored backups are deleted. Exported copies must be removed from their chosen destinations separately.

The current app does not create an account on a WalletSlice-operated server. A Google account used for backup remains your Google account; clearing WalletSlice data does not delete it. Contact the privacy address for questions or deletion requests concerning support correspondence.

Audience and changes

WalletSlice is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Our initial launch is in India. It is not directed to children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate. Financial records stored on a phone or in a user's Google Drive remain under that user's control; we cannot remotely inspect or erase them.

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and update its effective date. Where a feature needs a new disclosure or consent, WalletSlice will present it before enabling that data use.
