Android Personal Finance App

Your money. Clearly organised.

Track spending. Plan budgets. Stay on top of bills.

WalletSlice is a personal finance and budget tracking application for Android published by ccm. It helps you record expenses, manage budgets and bill reminders, and optionally back up your financial records directly to your own Google Drive.

Why WalletSlice

Private & offline-first

Your transactions, budgets, and notes stay on your Android device — no developer servers, no ads, and no usage tracking.

Smart expense & bill capture

Optionally detect banking and payment notifications on-device to review expenses and set credit card bill reminders, or log everything manually.

Budgets & credit tracking

Organize spending by category, track credit limits, and stay ahead of upcoming card statements and bills.

Optional Google Drive backup

Automatically back up and restore your data using your own hidden Google Drive application data folder (appDataFolder) — WalletSlice never requests access to your other Drive files.

Device lock protection

Keep your finances private with optional Android biometric or screen-lock authentication handled directly by Android.

Google Drive Backup & Data Privacy

WalletSlice requests access only to the narrow Google Drive application-data folder (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata) when you choose to enable cloud backup and restore. Your backups stay inside your own Google account and are never sent to developer-operated servers.

WalletSlice's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Read our full Privacy Policy for details on notification access, backups, retention, and deletion.
